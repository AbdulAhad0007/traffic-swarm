## God's Eye View
The 3D View's rendering approach draws on techniques from God's Eye View: updating batched primitives in place, rendering on demand, altitude and quality-based detail, and explicit layer ownership and disposal. Its code is not installed as a dependency, and none of its datasets or 3D models are used.
